## Description (4000 chars max)

```
Salat Times gives you Islamic prayer times calculated directly on your device from your location — no account, no ads, no tracking. Times are astronomical calculations and can differ by a few minutes from your local mosque, so confirm with them if in doubt.

PRAYER TIMES AT A GLANCE
• All six daily times: Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha
• Live countdown to the next prayer, updated every second
• Clear highlighting of the current and upcoming prayer
• Time zones and daylight saving changes handled correctly
• Hanafi or Standard Asr, and automatic high-latitude handling for northern summers

ADHAN & REMINDERS
• Optional adhan sound or standard notification for every prayer
• Per-prayer control: on/off, at prayer time or 5–30 minutes before
• Reminders are scheduled a week ahead and refreshed in the background when iOS allows — open the app now and then to keep them current

QIBLA COMPASS
• Animated compass needle pointing toward the Kaaba in Mecca
• Pure astronomical calculation; works without an internet connection

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• Small widget: the next prayer and its time
• Medium widget: the full day at a glance
• The "Next" highlight flips at the exact minute of each prayer

CALCULATION METHODS
Choose the convention used in your region:
• Muslim World League
• Egyptian General Authority
• University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi
• ISNA (North America)
• Umm al-Qura (Makkah)
• Diyanet (Turkey)
• Singapore / Malaysia / Indonesia
• France (UOIF)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Prayer times are calculated on-device and your location is not sent to us. The city name is looked up with Apple's location services. No data is collected, no analytics, no tracking — see our privacy policy.

Salat Times is designed to be the app you glance at five times a day: fast, beautiful, and reliable.
```
