# Privacy Policy — Salat Times

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026

Salat Times is built around a simple principle: your data stays on your device.

## Location

Salat Times uses your device's location for exactly two purposes:

- calculating Islamic prayer times for where you are, and
- pointing the Qibla compass toward the Kaaba.

All prayer-time and Qibla calculations happen **entirely on your device** using astronomical formulas. Your location is never sent to us or to any third-party service run by us, and never used for advertising or analytics.

To show your city name, the app asks Apple's location services (reverse geocoding) to turn your coordinates into a place name; this is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you are offline, the city name may not appear but prayer times still work.

The app saves your last known location, calculation settings and reminder preferences **on your device only** (in the app's local storage and an app group shared with the widget) so reminders and the widget can refresh in the background. Deleting the app removes this data.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

## Data collection

Salat Times collects **no data**: nothing is sent to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party services. Prayer times are estimates; please confirm with your local mosque if in doubt.

## The widget

The home screen widget reads prayer times and your city name from storage shared with the app on your device only.
