# Privacy Policy — Salat Times

**Effective date:** August 28, 2026

Salat Times is built around a simple principle: your data stays on your device.

## Location

Salat Times uses your device's location for exactly two purposes:

- calculating Islamic prayer times for where you are, and
- pointing the Qibla compass toward the Kaaba.

All calculations happen **entirely on your device** using astronomical formulas. Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party, never stored on any server, and never used for advertising or analytics.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

## Data collection

Salat Times collects **no data whatsoever**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party services. The app works fully offline after your location is determined.

## The widget

The home screen widget reads prayer times from shared storage on your device only.
